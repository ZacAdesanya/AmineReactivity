# Proton Transfer Explorer
## Proton Transfer Trends Across Amine–Lignin Monomer Complexes

The dataset covers 90 xTB NEB-TS minimum-energy-path (MEP) trajectories for proton transfer from a lignin-related proton donor to one of six amines, spanning seven donor systems: Water, sinapyl alcohol, guaiacyl, hydroxyphenyl, cinnamyl alcohol, ferulic acid, and glucuronic acid. Each trajectory starts at the neutral hydrogen-bonded complex and ends at the proton-transferred (ammonium/anion) ion pair; relative energies are reported in kcal/mol along 10 interpolated frames per path.
